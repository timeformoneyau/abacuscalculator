Assumptions & limitations

  • Acquisition/estimate tool: intentionally simpler than full serviceability.
  • No assessment-rate buffer applied (uses product rate directly) — same for both funders.
  • Tax = FY2026/27 resident scale; Medicare thresholds = FY2025/26 (single only) — flagged mismatch.
  • "Other income" is a single lumped field; real policy shades by income type. Modelled as the variable-income treatment (Bendigo 80% / ColCap 100%).
  • Rental income is NOT separately modelled (falls in "other income").
  • ColCap HEM uses the single "Australia" table (Q2 2025) as a placeholder; region-based HEM not supported.
  • Several ColCap constants remain to be confirmed with credit policy. QMHL-vs-Homestar ColCap policy parity is an open question (owner: Scott Sommer).
